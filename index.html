Timeless Village Charm Meets Mountain Tranquility

Nestled among the terraced green hills of Kavre, Dhulikhel Village Resort offers a tranquil sanctuary away from the hustle of city life.

Crafted with exposed Newari brickwork, handcrafted woodwork, and stone-paved walkways, our resort lets you witness the sunrise over the Langtang and Dorje Lakpa ranges from your private balcony.
